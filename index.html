Our approach

One focused catalog—not an open marketplace.

The OptiFlow catalog will be assembled product by product. Exact items and variants are identified before they are offered.

What this means

Visitors see an intentional selection of pre-identified products, rather than unrestricted access to a third-party catalog.

  1. 01

    Identify

    Select the exact product, brand, size, and variant intended for the catalog.

  2. 02

    Review

    Evaluate product information, labeling, seller and fulfillment requirements, and catalog fit.

  3. 03

    Present

    Prepare clear product information so each item is easy to understand and compare.

  4. 04

    Monitor

    Pause availability when product, cost, inventory, or fulfillment conditions no longer meet policy.

Launch standards

Designed for clarity and control.

◆

●

Availability safeguards

○
